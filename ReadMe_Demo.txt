STRONKIE'S OLYMPICS - FREE DEMO
Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft (c) 2026

This demo has the opening ceremony and one event: SOCCER, a penalty
shoot-out against another country. Make your own athlete (name,
country, man or woman, skin and hair), shoot your penalties and keep
goal against theirs, and win your medal.

The full game has six events on 2 disks: soccer, basketball,
volleyball, bowling, surfing and skateboarding, plus the closing
ceremony: https://stronkie.itch.io/

Needs an Amiga 500 with Kickstart 1.3 and 1 MB (512K chip + 512K
trapdoor), a joystick in port 2 (or the cursor keys and RETURN).
