STRONKIE'S OLYMPICS - the sports game for the Amiga 500
Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft (c) 2026

SIX EVENTS, EACH LOADED FROM DISK WHEN IT STARTS
  Soccer         a penalty shoot-out against another country
  Basketball     the three point shoot-out: 25 balls, 60 seconds
  Volleyball     beach volleyball, 1 against 1, first to 7
  Bowling        ten pin bowling, 10 frames
  Surfing        ride the wave: turns, airs and spins
  Skateboarding  the half pipe: pump, fly, turn, grab and spin
  With an opening ceremony (the athletes march in, the flame is lit,
  the doves fly off) and a closing ceremony at night.

MAKE YOUR OWN ATHLETE
  Your name, your country (20 countries: your kit gets its colours),
  man or woman, skin and hair. Up to 4 players take turns. In every
  event you meet 3 athletes from other countries: gold, silver and
  bronze for the best 3, a medal table at the end, and world records
  that are saved on disk 1.

WHAT YOU NEED
  An Amiga 500 (any Amiga with a 68000) with Kickstart 1.3 and 1 MB:
  512K chip + 512K trapdoor. A joystick in port 2 (or the cursor keys
  and RETURN). ESC gives up an event, M switches the music off.

THE DISKS
  The full game comes on 2 disks: boot disk 1 (Olympics1); the game
  asks for disk 2 (Olympics2) when bowling, surfing or skateboarding
  start, and for disk 1 again when it needs it. On a Gotek: switch
  the disk image when the game asks.
  Hard drive / CF card: unpack the hard drive zip (all files in one
  drawer) and double click SOlymp. WHDLoad: see the WHDLoad zip.

THE MUSIC
  All the music is written for this game by Stronkie Soft (it is
  played quietly, so the effects and the voices can be heard).
