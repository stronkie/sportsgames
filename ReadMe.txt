STRONKIE'S OLYMPICS - the sports game for the Amiga 500
Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft (c) 2026

18 EVENTS, EACH LOADED FROM DISK WHEN IT STARTS
 The first six
  Soccer           a penalty shoot-out against another country
  Basketball       the three point shoot-out: 25 balls, 60 seconds
  Volleyball       beach volleyball, 1 against 1, first to 7
  Bowling          ten pin bowling, 10 frames
  Surfing          ride the wave: turns, airs and spins
  Skateboarding    the half pipe: pump, fly, turn, grab and spin
 The classic Olympic events
  100m Sprint      against a runner from another country
  Hurdles          110 metres, 10 hurdles
  Long Jump        run, jump from the board, the best of 3
  Javelin          run, throw at the line, the best of 3
  Archery          6 arrows, a swaying sight and the wind
  Swimming         50m freestyle: dive, swim, breathe
  Weightlifting    clean and jerk, 30 kg more after a good lift
  Cycling          500m on the velodrome, with 5 gears
 The silly events
  Root Beer Sprint drink, run, don't hiccup
  Pie Dodge        cross the arena while the clowns throw pies
  Penguin Curling  slide penguins to the house, sweep!
  Toilet Paper Throw  the javelin with a toilet roll (and wind)
 Play all 18, the 14 classic ones, the 4 silly ones, or choose.
 With an opening ceremony (the athletes march in, the flame is lit,
 the doves fly off) and a closing ceremony at night.

MAKE YOUR OWN ATHLETE
  Your name, your country (20 countries: your kit gets its colours),
  man or woman, skin and hair. Up to 4 players take turns. In every
  event you meet 3 athletes from other countries: gold, silver and
  bronze for the best 3, a medal table at the end, and world records
  that are saved on disk 1.

WHAT YOU NEED
  An Amiga 500 (any Amiga with a 68000) with Kickstart 1.3 and 1 MB:
  512K chip + 512K trapdoor. A joystick in port 2 (or the cursor keys
  and RETURN). ESC gives up an event, M switches the music off.
  HOW TO PLAY in the menu explains every event.

THE DISKS
  The full game comes on 3 disks: boot disk 1 (Olympics1).
    Disk 1: soccer, basketball, volleyball, bowling, surfing
            (and the ceremonies and the world records)
    Disk 2: skateboarding and the other events
    Disk 3: the toilet paper throw
  The game asks for the disk it needs. On a Gotek: switch the disk
  image when the game asks.
  Hard drive / CF card: unpack the hard drive zip (all files in one
  drawer) and double click SOlymp. WHDLoad: see the WHDLoad zip.

THE MUSIC
  The music is written for this game by Stronkie Soft (it is played
  quietly, so the effects and the voices can be heard). At the medal
  ceremony you hear the national anthem of the gold medal winner:
  these old melodies are free to use, the arrangements are ours.
